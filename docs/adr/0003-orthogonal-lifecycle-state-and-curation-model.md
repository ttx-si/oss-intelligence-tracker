# Orthogonal Lifecycle State and Curation Model with Hybrid Cold-Start Baselines

Treating `CURATED` as a peer state alongside `DISCOVERY`, `SURGING`, `SUSTAINED`, and `COOLED` overwrites a repository's real-time momentum signal the moment it is featured in HelloGitHub. We decided to decouple momentum from editorial endorsement into two orthogonal dimensions: `lifecycle_state` (`DISCOVERY | SURGING | SUSTAINED | COOLED`) and `is_curated` (boolean plus issue metadata). To prevent early-run failures and niche-language false positives, `SURGING` requires a daily star delta of at least `150` (or `50` per 6h) when a language has fewer than 10 historical samples or `<7` days of history, switching thereafter to `MAX(language_daily_mean * 3, 50)`. Every batch run evaluates all non-`COOLED` repositories across the full table, transitioning any repository unseen in trending signals for 14 consecutive days to `COOLED` while allowing re-entry to `DISCOVERY` or `SURGING` upon future spikes.
